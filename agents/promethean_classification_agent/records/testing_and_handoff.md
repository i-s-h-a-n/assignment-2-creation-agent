## Method and scope

On 19 October 2026, the frozen builder generated five prompt packets. The assistant reviewed the primary packet and all case contexts, then authored structured responses in the same Codex conversation. This is a model-assisted worked evaluation with shared context, not a blinded, repeated or independent model experiment. Source checks and mechanical validation are separately recorded. No real lender data, production test or measured ROI is represented.

## Evidence for and against

For the higher classification: the course's draft-production mechanism offers a plausible path to broader cognitive access, and research in customer support and consulting demonstrates useful capability. Against: these are bounded assistance results, not demonstrated removal of verification/accountability or evidence of civilization-scale dependency. The lack of a quality-adjusted 10X comparison and durable cross-tier evidence is the strongest challenge to a higher label. This is an evidentiary gap, not proof that future Promethean change is impossible.

## Team integration rule

Never convert broad technological importance directly into local adoption approval. Require a separate application assessment with an owned baseline, verified data permissions, measurable quality and benefit, and consequences appropriate to the organization. Keep the common output keys unchanged. Maturity, diffusion, financial viability and legal authorization remain other specialists' responsibilities.

## Remaining limitations

The course framework is interpretive and the tier distinction is contestable. The source set is deliberately bounded and historical, not a comprehensive October 2026 technology survey. Two work settings are not proof of two societal tiers. All organizations are hypothetical. Human review effectiveness and local benefits are unknown. Same-conversation examples can inherit the author's assumptions; fresh independent runs and a skeptical human review remain necessary before relying on the agent operationally.
