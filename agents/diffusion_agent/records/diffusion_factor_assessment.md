Compatibility is the leading local inhibitor because this high-consequence application depends on governed source use. Complexity is a supporting inhibitor. This ranking is a reasoned hypothesis to test, not a general RAG fact.

Potential change agents are research managers and the data team. Trusted senior analysts could act as opinion leaders through demonstrations; actual influence has not been measured. Provider implementations make the approach easier to encounter, but a vendor account has selection and commercial bias. Complementary assets include corpus rights, permissions, retrieval skills and evaluation capacity. No mandatory RAG-specific standard was verified.

General diffusion judgment: documented implementations extend beyond research-only availability. A precise Rogers stage for all RAG or midsize financial-research peers remains unestablished. Innovator/early-adopter descriptions are qualitative possibilities; early/late-majority and laggard shares require evidence not available here.

For the same six-month horizon, aggressive posture favors earlier controlled learning, fast-follower posture favors reference gathering before evaluation, and conservative posture favors offline preparation and reassessment before an analyst-facing pilot. The evidence and minimum control conditions do not change.
