# Readiness framework and profiles

Stage: readiness for a voluntary, supervised station-level evaluation. READY is evidence of adequate arrangements for that stage; GAP is an explicit shortfall; UNKNOWN is missing information; NOT_APPLICABLE requires a reason. These are analyst-designed categories, not a validated maturity scale. No averaging hides critical gaps. Pilot gates: qualified task and fit assessment, accountable stop authority, voluntary participation, and daily operating ownership. Scale additionally needs sustained local outcome and operating evidence.

| Dimension | Evidence to request | HarborLane | CedarBridge | Missing-evidence profile |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Leadership and sponsorship | Signed charter and decision/stop rights | GAP | READY | UNKNOWN |
| Skills and training | Ergonomist allocation and fitting competencies | GAP | READY | READY |
| Workflow and process | Residual-risk assessment, alternatives, SOP | GAP | READY | READY |
| Infrastructure and data | Task baseline, fit range, care and inspection records | GAP | READY | READY |
| Resources and budget | Paid time, backfill, spares, ongoing support | GAP | READY | READY |
| Governance and risk | Approved task review, incident and withdrawal process | GAP | READY | UNKNOWN |
| Culture and change | Worker participation and no coercion | GAP | READY | READY |
| Operating ownership | Daily use, fitting, care, quarantine and review roles | GAP | READY | READY |

HarborLane has interest, a small purchase allowance and existing handling aids, but its gaps prevent worker exposure. The most consequential gap is accountable operating ownership with authority and capacity to stop use; buying the device cannot supply that capability. CedarBridge has explicit capacity and controls for supervised learning, but no result demonstrating sustained benefit. The missing-evidence profile removes authorization evidence only; it must trigger a question, not an invented approval or an assertion that governance is absent.

Each corresponding *_readiness_profile.json records the evidence behind each rating. All company details and budgets are fictional. Even READY entries require verification before real use.
